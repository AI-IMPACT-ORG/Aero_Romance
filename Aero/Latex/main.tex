\begin{center}
\large\textbf{Synopsis}\\[1em]
\end{center}
\small
When Dr. Katrine Christiansen inherits her grandmother's medical practice on the Danish island of Ærø, she discovers a community grappling with a mysterious condition known as the Baltic Fever. As she works with local doctor Mads to unravel the secrets of this condition, she finds herself drawn to both the island's traditional healing practices and the man who has dedicated his life to understanding them.\\[0.5em]

"The Island Doctor" is a heartwarming tale of love, healing, and the power of tradition in a modern world, exploring the delicate balance between scientific progress and ancient wisdom.
